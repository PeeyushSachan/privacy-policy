1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SaveSure ("SaveSure", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the SaveSure mobile application.

SaveSure is designed to help users organize warranties, events, coupons, and related reminder information in one place.

By using SaveSure, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information You May Provide

Depending on the features you use, SaveSure may allow you to enter information such as:

  • Warranty information, including product name, brand, model, purchase date, warranty period, and expiry date.
  • Event information, including event name, date, time, location, notes, and invitation-related information.
  • Coupon information, including coupon name, discount or offer, coupon code, expiry date, store or brand, and coupon details.
  • Images or documents that you voluntarily add to your cards, such as product images or purchase receipts.
  • Reminder preferences and dates/times required to provide reminder functionality.

Please avoid entering highly sensitive information that is not necessary for SaveSure's functionality, such as passwords, payment card numbers, government identification numbers, or other confidential information.

3. Device Permissions

SaveSure may request device permissions that are necessary for features you choose to use. Depending on your Android version and enabled features, these may include:

  • Notifications: to deliver warranty, event, and coupon reminders.
  • Alarms/reminders: to schedule reminders at the times selected by you.
  • Display over other apps / reminder display permissions: where supported and required to display a prominent reminder experience.
  • Photos/files or camera access: only if the app provides a feature that lets you add images or documents and the relevant permission is required by your device.

You can manage permissions through your device settings. If you deny a permission, some related functionality may not work.

4. How We Use Information

Information handled by SaveSure may be used to:

  • Create and display your warranty, event, and coupon cards.
  • Calculate dates and remaining time for reminders.
  • Schedule and deliver reminders that you configure.
  • Display information you have saved when you request it.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the application.
  • Respond to support or privacy-related requests.

5. Local Storage and Cloud Services

Depending on the version and configuration of SaveSure, information may be stored locally on your device and/or processed using cloud infrastructure used to operate the application.

If cloud synchronization or other online functionality is enabled, information necessary for that feature may be transmitted to the relevant service provider.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Third-Party Services

SaveSure may use third-party services and software development kits (SDKs) to provide functionality, analytics, infrastructure, advertising, crash reporting, or other app services. These providers may process certain technical or usage information according to their own privacy policies.

Depending on the version of SaveSure, these services may include Google Firebase services and Google AdMob. Their use may vary by app version and region.

Third-party providers are expected to process information in accordance with their applicable policies and contractual or legal obligations.

7. Advertising

SaveSure may display advertisements through third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob, if advertising is enabled in the version you use.

Advertising providers may collect or receive information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction information, subject to applicable platform controls and the provider's policies.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be disclosed to:

  • Service providers that help us operate, secure, maintain, or improve SaveSure.
  • Advertising or analytics providers where those services are enabled.
  • Authorities or other parties when disclosure is required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect rights and safety.
  • Another party in connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law.
